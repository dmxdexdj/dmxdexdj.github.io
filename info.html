About Packet Park

Networking is one of those things that runs the whole world and almost nobody gets to see it working. This is my attempt at fixing that.

The goal

Make networking click for people who think it’s boring, with no jargon walls and nothing to install.

What’s inside

Everything on the main page is a toy you can play with. Jump straight to one:

  • Send a packetBreak routers, watch the detour
  • DNS and tracerouteHow names become addresses
  • NAT and CGNATWhy your devices have fake addresses
  • The labHTTPS, VPN, firewall, botnet, IPv6, Wi-Fi

Got an idea?

Good to know

Packet Park is a toy. The addresses are made up, the timings are invented, and real networks are far bigger and messier. It’s here to build intuition, not to replace a textbook.
