What we take on

Three areas where an outside view saves you the most flying hours.

Urgent parts sourcing

When an aircraft is stuck, we locate certified parts through our network of suppliers, check the paperwork, and arrange delivery to wherever your aircraft is waiting.

Maintenance planning

We schedule checks around your busiest routes and seasons, so the aircraft are in the hangar when flying is quiet, not when it counts.

Spares and fleet strategy

We work out which parts you should hold, which you can borrow or pool, and which aircraft in your fleet carry the most risk, then size your stock to match.

How an engagement runs

Most projects follow the same three phases.

1

Audit the fleet

We review your aircraft, maintenance records and past delays to see where time on the ground really comes from.

2

Rank the risks

Every part and aircraft is scored by how likely it is to fail and how many flights it would cancel.

3

Build the safety net

We set up backup suppliers, spares and maintenance plans for the handful of items that matter most.
